## 🎵 Chinook Music Sales Analysis

This repository contains an end-to-end data analysis project on the **Chinook Music Store database**, focused on understanding customer behavior, sales performance, and genre preferences in the physical music market.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this analysis is to generate actionable business insights to support strategic decision-making for Chinook's music sales. The project covers data understanding, data quality assessment, exploratory analysis, visualizations, and strategic recommendations.

---

## 🔍 Key Highlights

- Comprehensive data quality assessment ensuring reliable transactional data
- Analysis of customer demographics and regional distribution
- Identification of top-performing countries, genres, and artists by revenue
- Deep dive into customer purchasing behavior, churn, and retention patterns
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) and risk-based segmentation analysis
- Data-driven business recommendations to improve retention and revenue

---

## 📊 Tools & Skills Used

- SQL for querying and analysis
- Data aggregation and segmentation techniques
- Business-oriented data visualization
- Customer analytics and market insights

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Rock dominates sales across most regions, especially in the USA
- High CLV customers show strong loyalty and higher purchase frequency
- Certain regions show high churn, indicating the need for targeted retention strategies
- Genre preferences vary across countries, supporting localized marketing efforts

---

## 💡 Outcome

The analysis provides clear guidance on customer retention, genre bundling, regional focus, and personalized marketing, helping Chinook Music Store optimize revenue and long-term growth.
